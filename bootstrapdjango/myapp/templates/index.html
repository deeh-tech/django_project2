Our Story

Brewed for Good
Times and Great Pours

Founded in 2012, Deeh Breweries began with a simple dream: to craft beer that feels personal, bold, and worth sharing. Today, we brew small-batch favorites that bring neighbors, friends, and travelers together around the tap.

Locally Sourced Ingredients

We work with regional growers and maltsters to bring fresh, quality ingredients to every batch.

Award-Winning Brews

Our taps have earned multiple local and national recognition for consistency, taste, and character.

Slow-Brewed, Better-Poured

Every beer is brewed in small batches, fermented with care, and poured to highlight its full flavor.
